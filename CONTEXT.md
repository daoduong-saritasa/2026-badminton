# Badminton tournament

## Language

The interface is written in Vietnamese, so each term carries the Vietnamese word
the interface uses. Never translate a player, pair, or tournament name that
someone entered.

**Team** (_Đội_):
A four-player roster that competes together in the tournament, containing two
seed 1 and two seed 2 players. _Avoid_: "pair" for the four-player roster.

**Pair** (_Cặp_):
Two players from the same team selected to compete together in a doubles match.
_Avoid_: "team" or "đội" for the two-player pairing.

**Team fixture** (_Cuộc đối đầu_):
A contest between two teams, made up of doubles matches. A qualifying fixture
contains two matches and may end level; a placement fixture goes to the first
team to win two matches.

**Match** (_Trận_):
A doubles contest between two pairs. Qualifying and qualification playoff
matches are decided by a single game; in a placement fixture, the first pair to
win two games takes the match.

**Game** (_Ván_):
One points-scored contest within a match, played to the target and cap for its
stage.

**Organizer** (_Điều hành_):
The person who runs the tournament: sets it up, schedules matches, and records
or corrects results. _Avoid_: "ban tổ chức" and "BTC".

**Referee** (_Trọng tài_):
A person who starts an eligible match and records its score and game results,
without organizer authority.

**Qualifying stage** (_Vòng loại_):
The round robin in which every team faces every other team once, deciding which
teams reach the final and which play for third place.

**Qualification playoff** (_Trận tranh vé_):
An extra match played to separate teams the ranking criteria left tied for a
final place.

**Placement fixture** (_Cuộc đối đầu xếp hạng_):
The final or the third-place fixture. Each decides two finishing positions.

**Drawn fixture** (_Hòa_):
A qualifying fixture that ends level, each team winning one match. Only
qualifying fixtures can be drawn.

**Supervised draw** (_Bốc thăm_):
Deciding a playoff matchup by drawing lots in front of the participants, used
only when all four teams are tied. Advancement is never drawn; teams still tied
play on until a result separates them.

**Pair assignment** (_Xếp cặp_):
Setting which two players represent a team in one match, done by a referee or
the organizer after the team names them and before the match starts. Pairs are
never declared in advance. _Avoid_: "lineup", "đội hình", "substitution",
"thay người".

**Rule exception** (_Ngoại lệ_):
A pair assignment by the organizer that breaks the pairing rule of its stage,
such as a same-seed pair in qualifying when a player is absent. Only the
organizer can make one.

**Seed** (_Hạt giống_):
A player's classification, 1 or 2. It describes the player, not a team's
position in a draw. A pair combines one player of each seed in qualifying
matches and the first two third-place matches; the third-place decider, every
final match, and qualification playoff pairs may combine any two teammates.

**Rank** (_Thứ hạng_):
A team's position in the competition standings. Distinct from a seed.

**Walkover** (_Xử thắng_):
A match win awarded without play because the opposing pair cannot play, with no
artificial score. Earlier match results remain intact. Affects one match only,
unlike a withdrawal.

**Withdrawal** (_Rút lui_):
A team leaving competition permanently. This tournament does not use permanent
withdrawal; absences are handled per match through walkovers.

**Result correction** (_Sửa kết quả_):
A change to a match result that was already recorded. It rewrites one match and
recalculates what follows from it, unlike a reset, which clears every result.

**Reset progress** (_Đặt lại tiến trình_):
A tournament reset that clears play progress and returns to editable setup,
while retaining the tournament name, teams, players, seeds, and court count.
Fixtures and their court assignments and playing order are also retained.

**Reset all** (_Đặt lại toàn bộ_):
A tournament reset that clears both setup and play data, returning to empty setup.
